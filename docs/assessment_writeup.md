# Assessment write-up

The chatbot uses FastAPI, a Next.js/TypeScript interface, local ONNX MiniLM embeddings, ChromaDB, and Google's Gemini API. Its knowledge base contains exactly the seven PlacidWay URLs embedded in the supplied assessment PDF. The crawler identifies itself, obeys robots.txt and the observed ten-second crawl delay, extracts article sections and price tables, then exports a portable snapshot. Vercel reconstructs its local index from that snapshot on startup. The public app is [placidway-ai.vercel.app](https://placidway-ai.vercel.app).

Accuracy is the main design choice. Exact price records retain the displayed currency, program type, duration, and “starting from” wording. A narrow comparison can show two exact clinic listings for the same procedure and explain that their services are not established as equivalent. Other factual answers use retrieved passages that Gemini selects but cannot rewrite or cite from outside the allowlist; a validator checks the selected text and source URL before display. Local checks block unsupported destinations, personal medical advice, privacy-sensitive messages, and false price premises. An unanswered question receives a clear limitation and a PlacidWay quote route where appropriate. Visitor names, contact details, and raw health narratives are not sent to Gemini.

The required eight assessment cases passed **8/8 against the live Gemini deployment** on October 28; the actual answers are in [the live test report](../evaluation/assessment_8_live.md). A separate local Gemini evaluation after the comparison refinement passed 30/30 cases, with actual outputs in [the Gemini report](../evaluation/full_test_results_gemini.md). Backend tests passed 88/88, and five browser tests passed after the readability update. These checks do not prove that every hidden question will be answered correctly.

Known limits: seven approved pages cannot answer the whole PlacidWay site; conservative matching sometimes declines a supported paraphrase; evidence excerpts may remain in English for Spanish or Arabic users; sessions, unanswered-topic logs, and refresh state are instance-local on Vercel. Durable content refresh requires exporting a new snapshot and redeploying. Lead capture was omitted to avoid collecting patient details in this assessment. The seven-day public-availability requirement cannot yet be verified because the cloud deployment began on October 28. With another week, I would review unanswered phrasings, improve multilingual evidence presentation, add durable privacy-preserving operations, and measure availability across seven days.
